Battle Church privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Battle Church is a browser game. This page explains what the game stores about you and why.

What we store

  • Your game progress: your save files, such as map progress, congregation and settings, so you can continue where you left off.
  • A sign-in cookie: a random code that keeps you signed in on this device. If you don't sign in, you play as a guest and the cookie links you to your guest progress.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture link. We use these only to sign you in, load your progress on any device, and show who is signed in. We never see your Google password.
  • Crash reports: if the game hits an error, it sends the error message, the page address and your browser type, linked to your player ID, so we can fix bugs.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No analytics services.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

Where it's kept and for how long

Everything is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts the game. Save data stays until you ask us to delete it. Crash reports are deleted after 90 days. Sign-in cookies expire after one year.

Deleting your data

To have your progress and account details deleted, email conradtolosa@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. If you played as a guest, clearing this site's cookies in your browser disconnects you from your guest progress.
